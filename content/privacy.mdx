Hack the Future Purdue ("HTF", "we") is a student organization at Purdue University. This policy explains what information this website and our application portal collect, what we do with it, who processes it for us, and how to reach us about it. It is written in plain language on purpose. [TODO: legal review]

## What this policy covers

This policy covers:

- this website, the pages you are reading now;
- the contact form on the [Contact](/contact) page;
- the application portal at `/apply`, once it launches (until then, applications go through an external form, see below).

It does not cover other websites we link to, such as Instagram, LinkedIn or the nonprofits we work with. Those sites have their own policies.

## Information we collect

### When you visit the site

The site uses Vercel Web Analytics to count page views. It does not use cookies and does not track you across other sites: it records the page visited, the referring site, and coarse device and location information (browser, operating system, country), and identifies a visit for the day with a hash that is not stored for longer. [TODO: confirm once analytics are switched on in the Vercel project, and link Vercel's privacy policy.] We do not run advertising trackers, and the site sets no cookies of its own.

Our hosting provider, Vercel, keeps standard server logs to run and secure the service: the IP address, browser type, pages requested and timestamps. We do not use these logs to identify individual visitors.

### When you use the contact form

If you send a message through the Contact page, we receive the details you enter: your name, your email address, whether you are a student, a nonprofit or someone else, and the message itself. We use them only to read and answer your message.

Depending on how the form is set up, your message is stored in our database (Supabase), sent to the club's email inbox (through Resend), or opened in your own email app, in which case your email provider handles delivery.

## How we use it

We use the information above to:

- answer your messages;
- run recruitment: review applications, schedule interviews and tell you the outcome;
- keep the site and portal secure and working;
- keep simple aggregate numbers, for example how many people applied in a cycle.

We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

## Security

Access to contact messages and applications is limited to the exec board members who need it. The services above use encryption in transit (HTTPS) and, for stored data, at rest. No system is perfectly secure, so if you believe your information has been exposed, tell us and we will investigate.
