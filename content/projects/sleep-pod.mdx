## Overview

Sleep Pod provides insulated sleeping pods for people experiencing homelessness and displacement, offering warmth, safety, and dignity. With just five full-time staff, it partners with prisons and local groups across the UK to produce 200–300 pods a month while creating rehabilitation and skill-building opportunities.
