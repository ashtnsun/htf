# home/

## `group-photo.jpg` — the club photo

One file, two places:

- the **home hero** (`PhotoHero`), full width of the screen behind the headline, darkened
  toward the bottom;
- the **mission block on /about**, cropped to 4:3.

Landscape, **2560 × 1440 or larger**. Everyone should sit in the middle band of the frame:
the hero crops the sides on a tall screen and the About crop takes a 4:3 slice, and the
bottom third of the hero sits under the headline and the buttons.

Fills the `org.group-photo` key.

> If the hero and the About block should ever be different photos, drop a second file as
> `hero.jpg` here and ask for a separate `org.hero-photo` key — the site supports it, the
> content just does not use it yet.
