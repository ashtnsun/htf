# projects/

One folder per project, named for the project's slug — the `slug:` in its
`content/projects/<slug>.mdx`, which is also the URL: `/projects/<slug>`.

Inside each folder:

- `cover.jpg` — the picture on the project card and at the top of the project page.
  Landscape **16:9**, 2400 × 1350 or larger. The card crops it to 4:3, and the featured card to
  a tall 4:5, so keep the subject in the middle square and away from the edges.
- `gallery-1.png`, `gallery-2.png`, `gallery-3.png`, … — screenshots of the thing the team
  built, in the order they should read. Tiles are **16:10**, so 1920 × 1200 or larger; clicking
  one opens it whole, so the full screenshot is what people end up looking at. `.png` for
  anything with text in it.

Covers and screenshots both need alt text and captions in the project's `.mdx`, plus the real
title, nonprofit, summary, stack and team — an image alone does not finish a project page.

The eight folders here are the 2025–26 projects. When a project is renamed, rename its folder
at the same time as the `.mdx` file.
